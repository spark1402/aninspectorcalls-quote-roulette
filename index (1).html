Quick revision reminders

Why is Eva Smith a character filter?

Eva never appears on stage and has no spoken lines. Selecting Eva shows quotations spoken about her or her treatment. Always attribute each quotation to its actual speaker.

1912 versus 1945

The play is set in 1912, before the First World War, but was first performed in 1945, after the Second World War. Priestley uses dramatic irony to challenge the complacency of wealthy Edwardians and encourage post-war audiences to consider a fairer society.

Priestley's message

Priestley supported greater social responsibility and a more equal society. The Inspector's speeches challenge selfish individualism, while the Birlings expose the dangers of class privilege.

How to build an analytical paragraph

Make a clear argument; embed a short quotation; zoom in on a precise word, technique or dramatic choice; explore its effect; link to Priestley's message and relevant context.

Assessment objectives

AO1: a thoughtful argument supported by references. AO2: analysis of language, form and structure. AO3: relevant links between the text and its contexts. Exact weighting depends on the exam specification.
